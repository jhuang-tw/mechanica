# Mechanica: 30-second pitch

For a live round or a spoken intro. The full submission text is in `SUBMISSION.md`.

> You learn how a machine works by taking it apart, and most people never get to.
> Mechanica puts eight real machines on your phone or in a Quest headset, and Meta Muse Spark is the teacher.
> A broken machine runs in front of you. You guess what's wrong, and Muse Spark checks your guess by moving the real parts with tool calls: it isolates the part, runs the drive, turns the shell to glass.
> Tap once and Muse directs its own narrated teardown. Photograph a real bike, and SAM 3.1 pulls each part out of the photo into the 3D model.
> Muse Spark, SAM 3.1 and Muse Voice Transcribe, in one web app, built entirely during this hackathon.

## Three points to remember
1. **The model acts, not just talks.** Muse Spark drives the simulation through validated tool calls.
2. **Learning by fixing.** Guess, get a clue, repair, explain why, get graded.
3. **Phone first, headset ready.** Same URL in a phone browser and in WebXR on Meta Quest, nothing to install.
